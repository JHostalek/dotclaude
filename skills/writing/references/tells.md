# Tells

Each entry names a tell, shows it, and gives the fix. Judge every hit against the original and the medium: a pattern the original's human author used on purpose is not a tell.

## Content

- **Invented substance.** A fact, number, example, or source absent from the original and the conversation. Fix: remove it. Worst tell in the catalog.
- **Dropped fact.** A fact, qualification, link, or identifier from the original missing or changed in the draft. Fix: restore it as the original states it.
- **Added scope.** Next steps, recommendations, offers, caveats, or recaps nobody asked for. Fix: cut.
- **Placeholder.** `[datum]`, `[name]`, "XX". Fix: the real value, or ask.
- **Reassurance.** Comfort or validation the facts do not support: "Vaše schránka je v bezpečí", "obojí je v pořádku", "Je skvělé, že to řešíme". Fix: cut.
- **Significance inflation.** "plays a key role", "a testament to", "marks a shift". Fix: state the consequence, or cut.
- **Trailing analysis.** A participle clause commenting on its own sentence: "..., highlighting the need for", "..., ensuring smooth operation". Fix: cut, or make it a claim with a reason.
- **Vague authority.** "experts say", "studies show", "best practice". Fix: name the source, or cut.
- **Promo voice.** seamless, powerful, cutting-edge, unlock, effortless. Fix: the concrete capability.
- **Placeless sentence.** It could appear unchanged in another project's text. Fix: this project's action, mechanism, or consequence.
- **Feeling for mechanism.** "SQL you can read". Fix: what it does, or a number: "`.toSQL()` returns the exact query".
- **Echo.** A sentence that restates the last one, announces the next one, or recaps. Fix: cut.

## Language

- **AI vocabulary.** delve, crucial, pivotal, robust, seamless, leverage, utilize, foster, enhance, landscape, underscore, showcase. In Czech: klíčový, zásadní, robustní, komplexní, efektivní, realizovat, zajistit, disponovat, provádět. Fix: the plain word.
- **Copula dodge.** "serves as", "stands as", "boasts", "features". Fix: is, has.
- **Metaphor noun.** substrate, vector, surface, north star, flywheel, paradigm, bedrock. Fix: the concrete word.
- **Mannered prose.** Aphorisms, proverbs, personified code ("the plan holds it"), figurative verbs ("rides along"). Fix: say it literally.
- **Flat voice.** A vivid or exact word from the original replaced by a generic one: "nezakuckal" becomes "bez jakýchkoli problémů". Fix: keep the original word.
- **Synonym cycling.** Several names for one thing. Fix: pick one and repeat it.
- **Emphasis adverb.** significantly, truly, really, jednoduše, opravdu. Fix: a stronger verb or the measurement. Adverbs that qualify meaning stay.
- **Hedge stack.** "could potentially", "it may be worth considering". Fix: the claim, with one hedge where the uncertainty is real.
- **Filler.** "in order to", "it is important to note that", "za účelem", "v návaznosti na". Fix: the short form, or cut. One "v rámci" is human, one in every sentence is a tell.

## Structure

- **Formulaic contrast.** "not just X, but Y", "it's not X, it's Y", "Nejde o to... Jde o". Fix: state Y.
- **Forced triplet.** Three items because three sounds complete. Fix: the natural count.
- **False range.** "from X to Y" with no scale between X and Y. Fix: list the items.
- **Rhetorical question.** A question the text asks only to answer it: "Znáte to?", "Proč to trvá?". Fix: state the answer.
- **Slogan close.** A punchy closing line that sums up or sells: "Žádné poplatky. Žádné háčky." Fix: end on the last fact.
- **Flat rhythm.** Consecutive sentences or paragraphs with the same length, shape, or opener. Fix: vary as the content allows.
- **List as answer.** Bullets for ideas that depend on each other, or headings and bold in a short text. Fix: prose.
- **Echo label.** A bold label repeating its line: "**Performance:** Performance improved", "**Kdy:** zítra". Fix: fold it into the sentence.
- **Colon lead-in.** "Regarding your question:", "The fix:", "Bottom line:". Fix: start with the point.
- **Hidden actor.** Passive where the actor matters: "queries are validated", "je prováděno", "doporučuje se". Fix: name who does it.
- **Compression.** Dropped articles, verbless fragments, arrows, "=", unexplained abbreviations. Fix: whole sentences.

## Conversation residue

- **Chat frame.** "Great question", "Certainly!", "I hope this helps", "Let me know if", "Díky za trpělivost!", praise, claims about the text's quality. Fix: cut.
- **Register swing.** Slang, jokes, or forced warmth after a request to sound natural. Fix: the register the audience expects.

## Typography

- **Unicode dash.** An em dash or en dash used as punctuation. Fix: a period, a comma, or a spaced hyphen.
- **Curly quotes.** Fix: straight quotes.
- **Semicolon.** Fix: two sentences, or a comma.
- **Decoration.** Title Case headings, emoji. Fix: sentence case, nothing.

Spaced hyphens and parentheses are human and stay.

## Czech only

- **Calque.** English structure in Czech words: "dává smysl", "vaše praxe", "adresovat problém", "na konci dne", "sáhnout po". Fix: what a Czech speaker says: "má smysl", "jak to děláte", "řešit problém", "nakonec", "použít".
- **Noun chain.** Verbs turned into nouns: "provedení kontroly nastavení", "zajištění realizace změny". Fix: a verb with an actor: "zkontrolujeme nastavení".
- **Ty/vy mix.** Ty and vy mixed, or "Vy" and "vy" mixed, in one text. Fix: one form, matching the original.
